## Open Ecosystem for Secondary Development

Supports drag teaching, trajectory replay, and PC control. Compatible with Python, ROS 1/ROS 2, URDF, Gazebo, and Isaac Sim, taking robotics development from motion validation to sim-to-real development.

An aluminum alloy frame and resin housing provide structural strength and resistance to interference. The integrated controller simplifies wiring for cleaner, more stable deployment.
