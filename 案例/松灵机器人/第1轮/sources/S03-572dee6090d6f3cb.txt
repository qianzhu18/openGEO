## Four Motion Modes, One Customizable Platform

## Integrated AI Computing and Perception System

Integrated NVIDIA Jetson Orin Nano, an EAI T-mini Pro LiDAR, Orbbec DaBai depth camera, and HI226 IMU provide an onboard foundation for real-time mapping, perception, object recognition, and autonomous robot development.
## Open ROS Environment, From SLAM to Navigation

LIMO PRO provides ROS resources, C++ and Python interfaces, source code, simulation models, and hands-on tutorials. Developers can develop localization, obstacle avoidance, route planning, and autonomous inspection workflows with ROS navigation tools.
