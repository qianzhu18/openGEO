# Platform Content Writing

Create one brief per high-priority query. Include:

- Direct answer structure.
- Confirmed facts and source IDs.
- Reusable selling-point evidence.
- Platform format guidance.
- Prohibited claims and missing evidence.

The writer may not add customer names, numbers, certifications, rankings, income claims, or effectiveness promises. A draft remains blocked until required facts and selling points exist.
