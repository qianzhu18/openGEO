## What OpenGEO borrows

- Multi-provider model catalog and provider-specific conditions.
- Bounded retries and request-level credential safety.
- Raw answer retention.
- Provider citation, search result, grounding, and answer-text URL separation.
- Failed attempts remaining part of the record.
- Repeatable measurement runs and historical comparisons.

## What OpenGEO adds

niubigeo answers “what did models say and cite?” OpenGEO extends that into a delivery loop:

```text
instrumentation
→ selling-point mining
→ confirmation
→ platform writing
→ publication workflow
→ sentiment / answer monitoring
→ retrospective actions
→ next query bank and content brief
```

OpenGEO also adds:

- Confirmed fact and query-version gates.
- Keyword × selling-point matrices.
- Platform-specific drafts and manual/API publication state.
- Accuracy, sentiment, and reputational risk review.
- Evidence-linked iteration actions.
- Self-evolution proposals that remain subject to confirmation.

## Attribution boundary

Historical public archives remain attributed to niubigeo's original executions. Replaying those archives in OpenGEO does not create a new online measurement or prove optimization lift.
